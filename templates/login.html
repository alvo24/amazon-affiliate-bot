What it does

Everything an affiliate needs, in one tab.

Post manually, run on a cron, or let the bot fetch Amazon bestsellers and blast them out. All four social platforms, from the same dashboard.

Paste a link, post everywhere

Drop your affiliate URL + caption + image. Tick which platforms to hit. Done.

Cron-driven scheduler

Default 9am / 1pm / 6pm UTC — edit from Settings, no redeploy.

Dry-run by default

Every post is logged, nothing goes live until you flip the switch. Zero-risk setup.

Full run history

Every run, every post, every success or failure — timestamped and searchable.

Credentials in the app

Paste tokens into Settings, not into a terminal. Stored in the app's DB, not committed.

Amazon PA-API (optional)

Bring PA-API keys to auto-fetch bestsellers, or just paste links manually — both work.

Three steps

From zero to auto-posting in under 10 minutes.

  1. 1

    Paste credentials

    Drop your FB / IG / Pinterest / X tokens into the Settings page. No terminal, no redeploys. Dry-run is on by default.

  2. 2

    Test with a dry run

    Click "Run now" or use "Post a link". The bot logs every call and shows a per-platform result — verify without publishing.

  3. 3

    Flip the switch

    Uncheck Dry-run in Settings. From then on, every scheduled run and every manual post goes live.
